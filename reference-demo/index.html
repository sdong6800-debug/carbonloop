Formulas. Manufacturing: gluelam/lumber = %×122/100, paper = %×500/100, sawdust/offcuts(burned) = %×30/100, offcuts(building material) = %×122/100. Release: sawdust/offcuts(burned) = %×234/100, paper = %×825/100, gluelam/lumber/offcuts(building material) = %×678/100. Sequestration = sum of that cycle's release terms. Remanufacturing = energy consumed × local grid intensity + distance × 0.015 kg CO₂/km. All terms are per m³ of timber.

Grid carbon-intensity sources. Country figures: lowcarbonpower.org (lifecycle gCO₂eq/kWh, 2024–25) and lowcarbonpower.org/Iceland. World average: IEA, Electricity 2025 (445 gCO₂/kWh, 2024). Converted to kg CO₂/MJ (÷3600) for the remanufacturing term.
